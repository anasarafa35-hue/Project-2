7 Wonders of the Ancient World

6) Mausoleum at Halicarnassus (Turkey)

The Mausoleum was built for King Mausolus. His wife ordered it. It honored his memory. Love inspired it.

The tomb included statues and columns. Artists from different places worked on it. Their designs were detailed. The structure was unique.

Greek and Egyptian styles were combined. This showed shared ideas. The building influenced others. It became famous.

Earthquakes destroyed the Mausoleum. Stones were reused. The name still exists. Its legacy remains.

Fun Facts

  • The word “mausoleum” comes from this tomb.
  • Each side was designed by a different artist.
  • A stepped pyramid stood on top.
