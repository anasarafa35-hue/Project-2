7 Wonders of the Ancient World

5) Temple of Artemis at Ephesus (Turkey)

The Temple of Artemis honored the goddess Artemis. It stood in Ephesus. It was one of the largest temples. Visitors were amazed.

The temple had marble columns and carvings. Artists worked for many years. Their skill was impressive. The design was detailed.

The temple was used for worship and trade. It became a social center. Religion and daily life mixed. Many people gathered.

The temple was destroyed many times. Only ruins remain today. Its history lives on. It is still remembered.

Fun Facts

  • It took over 100 years to build.
  • It stood on marshy ground.
  • It had over 100 columns.
