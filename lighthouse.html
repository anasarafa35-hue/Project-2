7 Wonders of the Ancient World

2) Lighthouse of Alexandria (Egypt)

The Lighthouse of Alexandria guided ships safely to the harbor. It stood on the island of Pharos. Sailors depended on it. Trade became safer.

Fire and reflected sunlight were used to create light. The lighthouse could be seen from far away. This was advanced technology. Many lives were saved.

The tower was tall and strong. Engineers planned it carefully. It survived storms for many years. Its design impressed others.

Earthquakes eventually destroyed the lighthouse. Nothing stands today. Its idea lives on. Modern lighthouses were inspired by it.

Fun Facts

  • It was also called the Pharos of Alexandria.
  • The tower had three levels.
  • A statue may have stood on top.
