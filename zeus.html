7 Wonders of the Ancient World

4) Statue of Zeus at Olympia (Greece)

The Statue of Zeus stood inside a temple at Olympia. It honored Zeus, the king of the gods. Visitors traveled far to see it. The statue inspired awe.

The statue was made of gold and ivory. Zeus sat on a large throne. The statue filled the temple. People felt small before it.

Olympia hosted the Olympic Games. Athletes believed Zeus watched them. Religion and sports were connected. The statue made the games sacred.

The statue was destroyed by fire. Nothing remains today. Written records describe it. Its influence remains.

Fun Facts

  • The sculptor Phidias created it.
  • Zeus held a statue of Nike.
  • Oil protected it from moisture.
